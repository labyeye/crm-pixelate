# **App Name**: Pixelate Nest CRM

## Core Features:

- Lead Management: Display leads in a grid of bordered cards with status tags and drag-and-drop functionality.
- Quotation Generation: Generate simple quotations with bold headings and clear sections.
- Client Confirmation: Allow clients to confirm or reject quotations via prominent call-to-action buttons and status labels.
- Project Management: List projects in a raw grid with oversized titles and flat progress bars for milestones.
- Invoicing: Generate invoices as plain documents with bold sections for client, services, and total, and payment status indicators.
- Support Ticket Management: Provide a ticket board with big text rows and SLA countdown blocks.
- Reports & Analytics: Display data through flat charts and massive bold numbers on a data wall dashboard.

## Style Guidelines:

- Background: Bright white (#FFFFFF) to provide a clean and contrasting backdrop.
- Primary accent: Bold blue (#2962FF) to highlight key interactive elements and sections, reflecting the reliable nature of a CRM. The choice of blue moves the brutalist styling away from aggression or industrial undertones.
- Accent: Yellow-orange (#FFAB40) to bring an optimistic contrast to statuses such as 'OVERDUE', as well as highlight calls to action
- Font: 'Space Grotesk' sans-serif for all text, ensuring a strong, modern appearance.
- Blocky sections with asymmetric grids and heavy borders (2-4px) to emphasize the light brutalist design.
- Minimal use of icons; focus on clear, oversized typography for labels and statuses.
- Hover states use bold color fills and inverted text to provide clear interaction feedback.
